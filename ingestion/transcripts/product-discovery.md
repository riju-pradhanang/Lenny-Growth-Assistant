Discovery is most useful when it reduces a specific uncertainty. Start with the decision you need to make, identify the riskiest assumption behind it, and talk to people who have experienced that problem recently. A good interview is not a request for feature ideas; it is an attempt to understand the current workflow, the workarounds, and the cost of leaving the problem unsolved. Teams should write down evidence separately from interpretation so that a confident opinion does not quietly become a fact.
